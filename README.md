# Recharge mode: ON

Free sleep tools for students aged 11–18, built from a survey of 62 students.

Live site: https://onedayatatime444.github.io/Bedtime-Information/

## Research question
To what extent does phone use in the hour before bed reduce sleep duration and next-day concentration among students, and can a simple curfew change it?

## What it includes
- Bedtime calculator and phone-off countdown
- Sleep score quiz and sleep debt calculator
- 7-day phone-curfew challenge
- Research page with the survey results
- Science page with the studies behind the project

## My role
I wrote the survey, analysed the 62 responses, and designed and built the tools and this website.

## Privacy
Everything runs in the browser. No accounts, and nothing is sent anywhere.
